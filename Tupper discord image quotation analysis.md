# Selective Quotation Analysis: The Tupper Discord Image (37:06–37:40)

**Scope:** What the video quotes from a November 2023 Discord exchange, what it omits from the same messages, and whether the omissions materially change how the quoted material reads.

**Source:** Screenshots of the original Discord thread, dated **11/20/2023**, obtained by community verification. Timestamps and message text are legible in the captures.

**What this document does not do:** it does not assess or characterise the image itself. The video's claim about the subject's appearance is contested and is not adjudicated here. The finding below concerns **omitted textual context** and stands independently of that dispute.

---

## 1. The video's claim

At **[37:06–37:40]** the video presents a censored version of an image shared by Tupper on Discord, describes it as one he called *"slightly lewd"* and *"a robot girl,"* disputes the robot characterisation, and concludes:

> "It's one thing to accidentally generate images, but to then casually post them in a Discord server proves Tupper's loyalty to the cause runs deeper than just ignoring the original Sipbox report from 2024."

Two things to note about the structure of this claim:

- The video **concedes accidental generation** and objects specifically to *posting*.
- The word doing the work is **"casually."**

## 2. The quotations are accurate

This is not a fabrication finding. Both quoted phrases appear verbatim in the 8:13 PM message:

> "Spoilering because it's *slightly* lewd (no NSFW bits, just a robot girl in underclothes […]"

The video quotes real words. The issue is what surrounds them.

## 3. What is omitted — five items, all from the same thread

### 3.1 The image was spoilered — and the quoted phrase says so

The full clause is **"Spoilering because it's *slightly* lewd."** The phrase the video quotes is Tupper's stated reason for **hiding the image behind a spoiler tag**.

This directly contradicts "casually post." A voluntary content warning is the opposite of casual posting, and the evidence for it is the very sentence the quotation is drawn from. The word "spoilering" appears immediately before the quoted words.

### 3.2 His own contemporaneous assessment is cut

> "(no NSFW bits, just a robot girl in underclothes […])"

The parenthetical qualifying the phrase is omitted. The video quotes "slightly lewd" and "robot girl" while dropping the assessment that sits between them.

### 3.3 He disclaims intent and attributes it to a tag artifact

> "I don't know why she's in a swimsuit now.. maybe the wading pool tag."

Posted in the same thread. An explicit statement that the output was not what he was aiming for, with a technical explanation.

### 3.4 He flags model drift as a problem — twice

**7:59 PM:** "It started giving me swimsuit robot girls so I had to pull it back but the water here was too good to pass up."

**7:59 PM:** "NAI tends towards lewdness if you're not careful 🫠"

"I had to pull it back" is a user identifying unwanted model behaviour and steering against it. "If you're not careful" frames the drift as a hazard to be managed. Neither appears in the video.

### 3.5 The conversation is technical throughout

Surrounding messages evaluate generation quality, not subject matter:

- "messy, got worse on upscale, but i really like this composition"
- "Super sketchy but again like the composition"
- "Also NAIv3 nails the hands. Sometimes messes up, but usually easily hits the mark."
- (reply) "hands in general have really improved"
- "It used to turn them into body horror but this looks great."

Hand rendering is the canonical AI image-generation failure case. This is an image-quality discussion among people comparing model outputs.

## 4. Effect of the omissions

| Video's framing | Same thread, in full |
|---|---|
| "casually post" | Voluntarily spoilered — the quoted phrase *is* the stated reason for spoilering |
| Quotes "slightly lewd" | Omits "no NSFW bits, just a robot girl in underclothes" from the same sentence |
| Implies deliberate sharing | "I don't know why she's in a swimsuit now.. maybe the wading pool tag" |
| Implies disposition toward such content | "I had to pull it back"; "NAI tends towards lewdness if you're not careful" |
| Implies casual approval of the subject | Thread evaluates composition, upscaling, and hand rendering |

**The quoted words are real. The disposition they are offered as evidence of is contradicted by the words removed from around them.**

## 5. Chronology

The exchange is dated **11/20/2023**. The Sippbox controversy runs **June 2024** — roughly seven months later.

The video's conclusion is that this post "proves Tupper's loyalty to the cause runs deeper than just ignoring the original Sipbox report from 2024." A message posted in November 2023 cannot evidence a disposition toward an event that had not yet occurred. The inference runs backwards in time.

## 6. Status and limits

**Verified:** the video quotes two phrases accurately while omitting five surrounding elements from the same thread, including the word immediately preceding the quotation, which contradicts its central characterisation.

**Not addressed here:** what the image depicts. That is disputed, it is not resolved by this document, and the finding above does not depend on it. Anyone citing this analysis should keep the two separate — the omission finding is clean precisely because it requires no position on the image.

**Not established:** intent behind the omission. Selective quotation can result from sloppy sourcing, from working off a second-hand crop, or from deliberate framing. Nothing here distinguishes these, and the document should not assert a motive it cannot evidence.
